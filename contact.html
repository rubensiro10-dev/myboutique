Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about orders, curtain measurements, and delivery timelines.

We believe in 100% customer confidence. You can place your order online with zero upfront cost. When our delivery partner brings your items, you inspect the quality, fabrics, and dimensions. Once fully satisfied, you pay via M-PESA or Cash.

Yes, absolutely! We offer custom drop-length tailoring, grommet installation, pencil pleating, and double-rod fittings. Just include your window measurements in the order form notes or send a photo on WhatsApp.

Delivery is FREE across Nairobi and Kajiado County (including Rongai, Karen, Langata, Kilimani, Westlands, and surrounding areas) for all orders over KSh 5,000. For smaller orders, delivery is a flat KSh 500. Upcountry parcel delivery is also available via trusted couriers.

We provide a 7-day exchange window for all non-customized beddings, carpets, and accessories as long as they are returned in original condition. For custom tailored curtains, our technicians work with you to adjust the hem or fit as needed.
